Overview

One look for every dialog, built on the CDK dialog, and a service that opens them and answers with Promises. alert tells the user something, confirm asks, and open shows a component of your own. None of them ever reject, a dismissed dialog (Escape, backdrop click, navigation) just resolves.

Setup

Dialogs render in the CDK overlay container, which needs its structural styles once. The dialog look itself is part of primitives.

Tones are info (default), success, warning, danger and error. danger is for a destructive action about to happen, its confirm button turns red and focus starts on Cancel. error is for something that just failed.

Your own dialogs

Lay a component out with the kit-dialog classes, name it with kitDialogTitle and close it with [kitDialogClose]="value" buttons, then open it with open(). The body scrolls while the header and footer stay in view, and the width is capped to the screen minus a margin.

Labels and translations

Titles and button labels have three layers, the closest one wins: English defaults, then app-wide labels (provideKitDialogLabels or provideKitLabels), then the call's own options. provideKitLabels('pl') switches every feature of the kit to Polish at once, and takes a signal of the language to follow one that changes at runtime. The kit has no i18n dependency, pass a signal to follow your own library.

API

Name Description
KitDialogService alert(), confirm() and open(), all returning Promises that never reject
KitDialogRef Returned by open(): result Promise, close(), componentInstance
KitDialogTitle [kitDialogTitle], marks the heading, styles it and makes it the dialog's accessible name
KitDialogClose [kitDialogClose]="value", closes the dialog on click and resolves result with the value
KIT_DIALOG_LABELS Signal of the default titles and button labels, English unless overridden
provideKitDialogLabels() App-wide override, merged over the defaults, takes a value or a signal
KIT_DIALOG_LABELS_EN, KIT_DIALOG_LABELS_PL Complete label sets to build on
provideKitLabels() One-call 'en' or 'pl' for every built-in string of the kit
.kit-btn--danger Button color modifier for a destructive action

Overview

Every native form control, shown as it renders in the dark and light scheme, the kit classes on each. What the kit leaves to the browser is noted where it comes up: the datalist popup, the image input and the output element, which have nothing of their own to style.

Headings marked kit are the controls that carry kit classes.

Setup

All of it ships in primitives, after the design tokens. Each control also has a module of its own to take on its own.

Text inputs kit

A kit-field wraps a label and its control, the label's for matching the control's id. The same markup serves every text-like type, the date and time ones below, and the states, which are the native attributes. An invalid value is marked with aria-invalid and explained under the field with kit-field__error.

@for (type of textTypes; track type) {
}

States

Invalid

An invalid field gets a 2px danger border, and a message under it: an icon and the text, never only a color. Mark it with aria-invalid="true" and point aria-describedby at the message. The browser's own validation shows the same look once the person has interacted with the field (:user-invalid, not :invalid, which would flag an untouched required field). A focused invalid field keeps the danger border and rings it in danger too, the pink ring would be too close to it.

Multi-line, choice and files kit

textarea, select, file and color take the same kit-field__control as a text input, and adapt to the element: a textarea grows with its rows, a select gets the kit's chevron, a file input its styled button.

Checkboxes, radios and switches kit

Classes on native inputs: kit-check__input inside a kit-check label. A switch is a checkbox with role="switch".

Fieldset and legend kit

kit-fieldset on the <fieldset> and kit-fieldset__legend on the <legend>, which keep their native job: the legend names the group for assistive technology and disabled on the fieldset disables everything inside. The fieldset spaces what is directly inside it, so fields need no margins. Add kit-fieldset--plain for a group without a border, whose legend reads as a heading.

Range, progress and meter kit

kit-range, kit-progress and kit-meter on the native elements. A range only knows its filled part if you tell it: bind --kit-range-fill as a percentage ((value - min) / (max - min) * 100). A progress bar is the violet fill, a meter takes its green, amber or red from where the value falls against low, high and optimum.

Buttons kit

kit-btn with a color modifier works on every native button-like element: <button>, a link, and <input type="button | submit | reset">. Hover and press darken the fill (the ghost button tints instead), and a disabled one ignores both.

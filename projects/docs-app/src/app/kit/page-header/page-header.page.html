Overview

The top of a page: breadcrumb, title and the page's main actions aligned to the right of the title. Plain classes, not a component, as what goes in it (the trail, the buttons, menus) is up to the app. Every part is optional.

Guidelines

  • One primary action per page, with an icon and kit-btn--lg, written last so it sits at the far right.
  • Up to two secondary actions, the rest in a menu behind an icon-only kit-btn--icon button.
  • Below 768px the actions move to their own row under the title and share it equally.
  • kit-page-header--sticky pins the header inside the shell's content area, bleeding out over its padding, so only use it there.

Styles

Part of primitives, or on its own:

Classes

Name Description
.kit-page-header The container, a grid of breadcrumb, title, subtitle and actions

.kit-page-header__actions The page's main actions, right-aligned, in DOM order

Overview

Non-blocking messages, where a dialog would be too much: KitToastService with success, error and info. Successes and information go away by themselves, an error stays until it is closed, and a toast with an action stays long enough to use it. Hover or focus pauses the clock. Needs the overlay styles and the toast styles, like the dialogs.

How long and where

4 s for a success, 5 s for information, until closed for an error, at least 8 s with an action. Change it for the app with provideKitToasts(), or for one toast with duration (a positive number of milliseconds, null for until closed). The labels, the region's name and the close button's, are in KIT_TOAST_LABELS.

Components and functions

Name Use
KitToastService success, error, info (message, {{ '{' }} duration, action {{ '}' }}) returning a KitToastRef, and dismissAll
KitToastRef closed, a Promise of 'timeout' | 'dismissed' | 'action', and dismiss()
provideKitToasts() App-wide durations, actionMinDuration, max and position
